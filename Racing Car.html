Select Game Mode

Casual Mode

Casual Mode helps players understand gameplay mechanics, controls, and movement. This mode is perfect for beginners and relaxed sessions where players focus on learning and improving personal scores.

Racing Mode

Racing Mode delivers a competitive F1-style experience where players race against AI opponents. Precision, overtaking strategy, and speed control are essential to secure victory.

Duration Mode

Duration Mode challenges players to score maximum points within a limited time. Collect time-extension blocks wisely to prolong gameplay and maximize scoring opportunities.

Infinite Drive

Infinite Drive offers endless gameplay without time pressure. Designed for stress-free sessions, this mode allows smooth driving and long uninterrupted play.

About Game

This game was created by INJ KRISH over six months for learning and education.

  • Casual Mode: Practice and training
  • Racing Mode: F1-style racing
  • Duration Mode: Time-based scoring
  • Infinite Drive: Relaxed endless driving

How To Play

  • N - Nitro Boost
  • Space - Reapply last move
  • Accelerator - Increase speed
  • Brake - Prevent blast (1s)
  • Arrow Keys - Car Controllers
